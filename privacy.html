Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Matched (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. We built Matched to respect your privacy: it is a single-player game that you can play entirely offline.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. Your game progress (your choices, in-game currency, and relationship status) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-party services

Matched is free and supported by ads. The following third-party services may process limited data under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process your purchases. We never see or store your payment details. See Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies.
  • Google AdMob — we show interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device advertising identifier (e.g. IDFA), approximate location (from IP address), device information, and ad-interaction data. On iOS we ask your permission before tracking; if you decline, you will still see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Children’s privacy

Matched contains mature romantic themes and is intended for users aged 17 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention & deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting your save from within the game or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
