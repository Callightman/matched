Frequently asked

How is my progress saved?
Your game saves automatically on your device. There’s no account to create.

I lost my progress / changed phones.
Saves are stored locally, so they don’t transfer automatically between devices. Email us if you need help.

How do gems and energy work?
Energy paces your day and refills when you sleep. Gems are an optional premium currency you can earn or purchase to unlock special choices and refill energy.

Is the game appropriate for all ages?
Matched features mature romantic themes and is rated for players 17+.

Restore purchases

If you reinstall the App, you can restore any previous purchases from the in-app Store screen.
