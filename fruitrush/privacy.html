Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Fruit Garden: Match & Farm (“the App”) handles information. The App is a single-player game that works offline.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The App has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules local reminders on your device (for example “your jam is ready”). They are created on your device; no data leaves it. You can turn them off anytime in your device settings.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. See Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the App deletes all of your game data.
