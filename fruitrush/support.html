FAQ

I bought something but didn’t get it. Open the Market and tap Restore Purchases. Basket packs are delivered right after purchase; if one was interrupted, it is delivered the next time you open the game.

Do I need the internet? No. The game works offline. Only purchases need a connection.

How do I get more baskets without paying? Harvest rounds, picking fruit from your trees, villager orders, the market cart and daily gifts all give baskets.
